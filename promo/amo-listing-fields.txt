===============================================================
Describe Add-on / Description
===============================================================
ZenQR generates and scans QR codes right in your browser. Your data is never sent to the developer or to any third-party service.

<b>Generate</b>
<ul>
<li>QR code for the current tab, a selected link or text, or anything you type</li>
<li>Templates for WiFi networks, contact cards (vCard), email, SMS, phone numbers, and calendar events</li>
<li>Batch-generate many QR codes at once, one per line of text</li>
<li>Adjustable error correction level, image size, finder/module style, and an optional embedded logo</li>
</ul>

<b>Scan</b>
<ul>
<li>Scan a QR code in any image from the right-click menu</li>
<li>Select any region of the page to scan</li>
<li>Paste an image from the clipboard to scan it</li>
<li>Scan with your camera (camera access is only requested if you use this feature)</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy and more</b>
<ul>
<li>History of everything you've generated or scanned, stored only in your browser, with import/export to a JSON file</li>
<li>No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Generating and scanning work offline; the only network request ZenQR may make is reloading an image you chose to scan from its original web address</li>
<li>Available in 10 languages</li>
</ul>

Open source (MIT license): <a href="https://github.com/zenLoading/ZenQR">github.com/zenLoading/ZenQR</a>
